About

I'm a master's student in Applied Data Science at USC, graduating in May 2027, with a B.Tech in Artificial Intelligence and Data Science from GGSIPU in New Delhi.

I like work that runs end to end: getting messy data into shape, training a model that holds up, and shipping it behind an API or a dashboard someone can actually use.
